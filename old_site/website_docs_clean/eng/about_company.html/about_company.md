# About  | Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP

Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP is one of the leading engineering and consulting companies in Oil and Gas Sector of the Republic of Kazakhstan, established and registered in the Mangistau Department of Justice of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Kazakhstan on 29th of August, 2003, # 5727-1943-TOO.

The profile of the company's activities includes work on the development of pre-design and design documentation of field development and covers:

- Design of oil, gas and gas condensate fields development;

- Design of main oil, gas and product pipelines and their facilities (oil pump stations, gas distribution stations and points);

- Design of auxiliary engineering systems (power supply, water consumption and water disposal, heating and ventilation, roads, infrastructure of rotational camps);

- Environmental support of projects;

- Approval of projects in supervisory bodies;

- Author supervision of facilities’ construction;

- Consulting services.

To implement services offered, the Company has all required licenses to perform works related to design of oil and gas facilities, to environmental design, to design of mining, petrochemical, chemical industries, underground facilities, tanks and pipelines.

The reputation of an honest, open Company that strictly adheres to high standards of corporate ethics is the key to our success and prosperity.
