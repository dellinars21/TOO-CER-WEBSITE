# Design | Services | Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP

The accumulated experience in the field of oil and gas facilities design, as well as the continuous improvement of the employees’ qualifications, the technical equipment of personnel, the use of the latest technologies and software allow the company to develop high-quality projects of all stages of design:

- Feasibility study of facilities’ construction

- Conceptual projects

- Architectural and planning assignment

- Project documentation

- Declaration of industrial safety

- Process regulations

- Detailed design

- Programs for the development and processing of associated petroleum gas

In the Company's asset - the development of concepts, programs, pre-project and project documentation in full compliance with the requirements of building regulations, rules and standards of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We perform engineering works for the largest subsoil users in Kazakhstan for such fields as Tengiz, Kashagan, Karachaganak, Alibekmola, Chinarevskoye, North Buzachi, Kalamkas, W.Prorva, Dunga, Arystanovskoye, Akshabulak, Karakuduk, Karazhanbas.

Among the designed facilities are oil treatment units (OTU) and gas (GTU), oil demercaptanization (ODU) and preliminary water discharge (PWD), pipeline transport systems and petrochemical facilities, infrastructure and offshore structures, gas re-injection and injection systems.

We provide technical support for the projects developed by us when passing the processes of obtaining approvals and expertises, we perform works on author's supervision.

Our projects allow us to maintain the image of a reliable partner, as evidenced by positive feedback from our Customers.
