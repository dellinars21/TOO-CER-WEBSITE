# Consulting services | Services | Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP

The scope of consulting services rendered by Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP includes work on verification of norms, standards and project documentation submitted by the subsoil user for compliance with the norms and standards of the Republic of Kazakhstan. As well as the participation of LLP specialists in developing projects by foreign design companies directly at design stage in accordance with the regulatory framework of the Republic of Kazakhstan, both with the provision of LLP personnel in the project offices of foreign companies, and remotely from our own office.

##### The specialists of Caspian Engineering & Research, LLP are ready to offer the best solutions in the following areas:

TECHNICAL CONSULTING AT DESIGN STAGE:

- Evaluation of technical documentation content

- Evaluation of project in terms of subsequent operation

- Consulting on selection of engineering equipment

- Recommendations for control diagrams of engineering systems

- Recommendations in design of initial and permissive documentation for construction of facility

TECHNICAL CONSULTING AT PREPARATION STAGE TO PUT FACILITY IN OPERATION:

- Recommendations in the preparation of required documents when passing the acceptance commission of constructed facility

- Support of construction and installation, commissioning works

- Organization and performance of complex tests of engineering systems and equipment of facilities

TECHNICAL CONSULTING AT FACILITY ACCEPTANCE FOR OPERATION STAGE:

- Participation in the Working Commission activities for facility acceptance for operation

- Facility acceptance for operation
